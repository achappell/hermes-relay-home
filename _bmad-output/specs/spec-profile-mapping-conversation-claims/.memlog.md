topic: Profile mapping and conversation claims
updated: 2026-09-13T19:41

- (direction) Slice B defines Home-owned Wake Mapping authorization, room-local wake claims, and Profile-bound conversation lifecycles on top of the existing foundation claim contract; it does not replace the foundation service or invent a Hermes wire channel.
- (decision) Use the existing v1 wake-claim contract as the wire starting point: an endpoint submits an opaque Wake Mapping ID with its authenticated device identity, never an arbitrary Profile ID, phrase, prompt, transcript, audio, or Hermes bearer token.
- (capability) CAP-1: Resolve household-wide Wake Mappings so each wake phrase has one authorized Hermes Profile meaning everywhere, while one Profile may have multiple distinct phrase aliases.
- (capability) CAP-2: Apply explicit per-device Wake Mapping grants, including a trusted approver's all-current-Profiles pairing shortcut, and keep those grants visible, narrowable, and revocable.
- (capability) CAP-3: Arbitrate simultaneous claims independently within each Room and grant at most one eligible device per wake using the existing bounded window, acoustic evidence, availability, and per-Room priority.
- (capability) CAP-4: Bind a granted device and Wake Mapping to one Profile and Hermes Session for an Active Conversation, then keep all non-empty follow-ups on that binding until close.
- (capability) CAP-5: Permit independent Hermes Sessions for the same Profile in different Rooms without joining, stealing, or retargeting; fail closed if a second independent Session cannot be created.
- (capability) CAP-6: Close conversations through a bounded idle window or explicit stop, disconnect, revocation, or expiry, while never expiring an in-flight turn, capture, or playback.
- (constraint) Wake phrase meaning is household-wide: duplicate or ambiguous phrases across Profiles are rejected before publication; the same phrase may be enabled in multiple approved Rooms or endpoints only for its one Profile.
- (constraint) Room-local arbitration uses Home's monotonic receive clock and the existing 250 ms window; a failed winner never promotes a loser and requires a new wake.
- (constraint) Wake Mapping edits are atomic for new wakes. A remap cannot change an Active Conversation's bound Profile or Session; explicit endpoint, Profile, or mapping revocation remains the hard-stop path.
- (constraint) Home remains authoritative for mapping freshness. Stale, removed, or unauthorized claims are rejected until refresh; Home never guesses a Profile or falls back to another phrase.
- (constraint) The all-current-Profiles pairing option is a snapshot at approval time. Profiles or Wake Mappings created later require explicit per-device authorization or another all-current approval.
- (constraint) A Room's winner is independent from other Rooms: the same phrase may produce one winner per Room, and a wake, failure, or active conversation in one Room cannot suppress, promote, or steal another Room's claim.
- (constraint) During an Active Conversation, a different wake phrase cannot retarget the binding; after close, a fresh wake creates fresh Profile and Session state.
- (decision) The idle timer starts only after response and playback complete, resets after each non-empty follow-up, and its exact duration remains configuration rather than a routing rule.
- (decision) Mapping changes do not interrupt active work by themselves; explicit revocation interrupts active work and invalidates late device messages.
- (decision) A custom wake phrase uses the same Profile isolation, device-grant, mapping-freshness, and fail-closed rules as a built-in phrase; detector implementation remains a later wave.
- (assumption) Endpoints perform local wake detection and have an authorized mapping snapshot; Home verifies the opaque mapping ID, device grant, Room, and current configuration before any capture or Hermes turn proceeds.
- (assumption) The existing foundation service can represent or create a Hermes Session through a later Home bridge adapter; Slice B specifies the binding and policy, not the final bridge transport.
- (question) What default idle duration and endpoint refresh mechanism should the implementation choose, and how should device Room reassignment interact with an active claim?
- (question) What exact Hermes Session creation and failure signals does the Home bridge expose when a granted wake cannot create an independent Session?
- (note) Traceability sources are the next-wave Home PRD FR-31 through FR-33 and SM-10, the next-feature slate Slice B, the Home foundation SPEC, the v1 contract README and schemas, and the Home architecture spine.
- (event) Slice B specification workspace initialized from the accepted household wake-routing decisions; capability IDs CAP-1 through CAP-6 are reserved for this spec.
- (constraint) Arbitration state must be partitioned by Room: unrelated Rooms may have simultaneous bounded claim windows, and one Room's pending round must never suppress or serialize another Room's wake.
- (constraint) An unavailable or revoked Profile or mapping fails closed and cannot fall through to another Profile; an endpoint may show the active Profile state without exposing a personal credential.
